# Cat Gesture Guide

The reaction is selected from `images/cat-reactions/` by its filename.

- `chill`: shaka (thumb and pinky extended)
- `thumbs-up`: thumbs up
- `pointing-up`: index finger raised
- `pointing-front`: index finger pointed at the camera
- `punch`: one closed fist; `mad`: two closed fists
- `heart`: index fingertips and thumb tips touching to form a heart; keep other fingers folded back
- `desperate`: two open hands raised high; `scared`: two open hands apart
- `disgusted`: index and middle fingers raised
- `sad`: one open palm
- `happy`: a wide smile; `tongue-out`: an open mouth
- `scuba`: one palm covering your mouth while the other hand is raised
- `sad`: both mouth corners turned down; `confident`: one-sided smile/smirk

Face-expression thresholds are approximate; tune them in `detect_gesture()`
after testing with your camera and lighting.
